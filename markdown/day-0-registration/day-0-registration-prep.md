# Day 0 Registration Prep

Points to Remember

1. Plan 1: Stack cushions close to reg table and ask each student if they got their own cushion. If yes, dont give them more. If not, give them an option of cushion/bench/wedge. Give away only one of these. Do not say anything about extra cushions on reg table. (Plan 2: Have cushion on each seat in the beginning itself and keep all extra cushions in office away from students sight. If questioned by student, inform them they will find a cushion on their seat in the hall. Keep extra cushions in next room to manager’s office away from students sight and should be handed off to students only by managers starting D1.)
2. Put 1 bottle of shampoo and 1 toothpaste tube with BAVC and do not remove label in each of the bathrooms. Whenever someone comes for these supplies, tell them to find in their respective bathhouses.
3. If students are taking benches, please take all but 1 cushion back from them.
4. There should always be 4 valuables boxes at women’s reg table and 3 at men’s. If you cannot find, contact coordinators. Do not try to fit stuff in less number of boxes.
5. Do not hand over alarm clocks in the beginning at reg table. Store them in office. When requested by a student, explain them how loud bells are and there should be at least one person in their dorm with an alarm clock which will help them to wake up. If this doesn’t work out, then they should see a manager. (Suggestion: When giving away alarm clock to student, if possible, managers can make a note of their dorm # and if someone else from same dorm inquires about alarm clock ask them to share it.)
6. Do not give away flashlights in the beginning on D0. Tell students that there is enough lighting on the path ways, if that’s still an issue after 2-3 days of course, please hand over them a flashlight.
