# Server FAQ

Occidental Setup Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring?

If you are planning to spend the night (which we recommend - since it’s far), please bring flashlights, blankets/pillows/sleeping bags. You’ll be assigned to a cabin, shared with bunk beds, for you to keep your stuff and spend the nights. Beakfast, lunch and dinner will be served during the set up days. If you need to wash clothes, please bring some laundry detergent so that you can handwash whatever needs cleaning.

Where?

CYO Retreat Center Address: 2136 Bohemian Highway, Occidental, CA 95465

Click here for [printable driving directions](http://www.manda.dhamma.org/reg/CYODirections.pdf).

Can I sleep at the center during the setup periods?

Yes. You’ll be assigned to a cabin to spend the nights of the Day -2, and Day -1. On Day 0 (day when students arrive) of the course you’re expected to leave that day after setup ends, unless you’re also serving/sitting the course.

What will I be doing?

There are many tasks that we need to do to make the space ready for a course. For example: setting up partitions to separate the meditation area from the kitchen area, setting up awnings at the entrances, covering windows with paper to prevent bright light from entering the meditation area, etc. You’ll be asked to help in areas based on the need and your ability.

Will we have group sits during the setup days?

Yes, 3 group sittings a day.

I need a ride to get to Occidental for set up / clean up…

We’ll go over ride sharing during orientation.  In the meanwhile, you can use this shared spreadsheet to connect and coordinate with other folks who are willing to offer rides.  Here’s the link:<http://bit.ly/occi-setup-rideshare>
