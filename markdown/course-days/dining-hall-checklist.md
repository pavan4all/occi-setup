# 11:00 Lunch Set-Up Checklist

1. Check that counters, tables (including outdoors,) and floor are clean
2. Re-stock/put out plates, bowls, cups, cutlery, napkins
3. Dish station:  Compost and trash containers.  Wash bin 2/3 full warm water & rack in bottom, plate, bowl and cup bus bins & 1 silverware container  ½ full of water,
4. Re-stock/put out the following:

Condiment tray: (2 of each)

- Honey – large honey pourer
- Sugar - white in pourers & brown in container with spoon
- Cinnamon – small shakers
- Cayenne – small shakers
- Gomasio – medium or large shakers
- Ground flax seed – medium or large shakers
- Nutritional yeast – medium or large shakers
- Soy sauce – small or medium pourers or squeeze bottles
- Oil and vinegar – squeeze bottles
- Sriracha Hot Sauce – use the 12 oz and refill from 32 oz

Main Course Table:  

- Check with cooks to find out what items are being served and in what order to place them on the buffet.
- Check if needed:  Butter and Earth Balance (take out ahead to soften,) sour cream and tofu sour cream, pickled vegetables, etc.
- Place rice last unless a stew or stir fry is served on top.
- Put signs from sign box in place: e.g.  “Dairy,” “Non-Dairy,” etc.

Salad bar:  For freshness, wait until 10:45 to take out of fridge

- On salad shelf in walk-in fridge

  - 2 of each: grated cheese, carrots, beets, cucumbers, tomatoes, raisins, sunflower seed
  - 1 of each:  Lettuce, sunflower soy dressing, Italian dressing
- Check with salad manager to see if there are any extra items

Tea/Coffee station:

- Instant coffee, instant decaf, Cafix, PG Tips, Tetley decaf., Bengal Spice, Celestial Seasoning Green Tea, Chamomile, Peppermint, Wild Berry Zinger, Caffeine Free, Lipton Tea
- Honey, sugars, lemon juice– see condiment tray above
- Milks: Wait until 10:55 – 2% & whole in thermos pitchers, soy and rice in small pourers.  

  - \*\*\*\*Empty and wash milk pitchers every day!\*\*\*\*
- Miso – small container with spoon (keep in the fridge)
- Beverages:  Iced tea, cold water, etc.
- Container of clean teaspoons
- Display Lunch Menu

# After Meal Clean-Up Checklist

1. Bring in and put away leftovers.  Medium to large quantities of leftovers must be immediately transferred to metal pans and placed on the cooling shelf in the walk-in fridge without lids.   (This rapid cooling retards spoilage.  See sanitation talk.)  Only after a few hours, when completely cold, can they then be stored in plastic, lidded containers.  Small quantities (2 quarts or less) can be put into plastic and into the fridge but leave the lid off for a few hours.  Label all containers on the side (not the lid) with the date (not course day number.)  Salad bar items should be consolidated and inventoried by the salad bar manager.  

3. After breakfast move bread from dining room freezers down into the fridges as needed for the next day.  Restock the dining room freezers from the bread freezer behind the kitchen if needed.  Return loaves that are less than half to server area for server use.

4. Clean up the dining rooms as follows:

- Wipe down counters/tables inside and outside
- Check napkin and towel supplies
- Sweep/vacuum if needed
- Straighten chairs
- Turn off lights/heat
- Take out trash when needed (at least once a day)
- Empty compost buckets, rinse and return
- Return cleaned dishes from the kitchen

\*\*\*\*Empty and wash milk pitchers every day!\*\*\*\*

5. Check bathrooms for cleanliness and supplies

6. Clean up the kitchen as follows:

- Wash dining room dishes and return to a clean cart for transport to dining rooms.  Blue dishes are female and white are male.
- Wash kitchen and server dishes, pots, and pans
- Scrape and wipe down all table tops and counters
- Clean/straighten server’s dining rooms and toaster area
- Sweep and spot mop, pulling up individual mats as necessary
- Once in the middle of the course take out all the mats and mop the whole floor.
- Clean stove top
- Clean up after ourselves
